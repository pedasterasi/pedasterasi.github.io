Starting somewhere: why I’m making games in public

This is the first note from a journey of learning, trying, changing my mind, and slowly making small games.

Every game starts in a slightly awkward place: a folder with too many test scenes, a character that slides when it should walk, and an idea that is clearer in feeling than in words. This is mine.

I’m making this blog to remember the process as it happens. I don’t want to wait until everything looks finished before sharing it. The small wins, confusing bugs, and half-formed ideas are part of the story too.

I want to make things small enough to finish, strange enough to remember, and honest enough to learn from.

What I hope to share

Some notes will be about game development. Others will be about things I’m learning from books, other games, or a day spent trying to solve one tiny problem. The important thing is that they are real notes from the work.

For now, I’m keeping the goal simple: make the next version a little more playable than the last one, then write down what it taught me.
