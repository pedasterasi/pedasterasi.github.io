Why I keep a devlog

Writing down the small changes makes an unfinished project easier to understand.

When I look at a project every day, it can feel like nothing is changing. A devlog gives me a way to look back and notice the things I would otherwise forget: the first working jump, a better colour choice, or a problem that took two days to solve.

It is not a report card. It is more like leaving small trail markers for myself.

A place to be honest

I want these notes to include the awkward parts too. The ideas that did not work, the time I changed direction, and the days when the best thing I did was take a break and come back with fresh eyes.

Making things is easier when I can see that every step, even the messy ones, is still a step.
